Model Evaluation

LLMs are non-deterministic, which makes evaluating the result complicated. A simple assert no longer works because it's very likely that the LLM returns a different response every time. And all or none of them might be correct and relevant. You could solve this by having a human evaluate the response. But that's, of course, not very practical.
